FAQ

What is the Cloudy AI Tool Finder?

A free 6-question quiz and directory that matches you with AI tools for coding, vibe coding, AI agents, game development, images, video, audio, writing, research and productivity. It covers 64 hand-picked tools.

How are tools ranked?

Each tool is scored on how well it fits your goal, skill level, budget, preferred workspace and priorities. Sponsorships and affiliate links never change the ranking.

What is the best free AI coding tool?

Good free starting points are GitHub Copilot Free, Aider (open source, bring your own model), Continue (open source) and the free tiers of Cursor and Devin Desktop (formerly Windsurf).

What is the best AI tool for vibe coding?

For no-setup browser building, try Lovable, Bolt, v0 or Replit. For bigger projects, Cursor, Claude Code and Codex give you more control.

Are the prices accurate?

Prices were checked against vendor pages and recent reviews (last reviewed 2026-11-10). AI pricing changes often, so always confirm on the vendor site.
